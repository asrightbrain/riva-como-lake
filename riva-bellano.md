# Riva — Bellano (prototype content)

Riva is a free app for foreign visitors to Lake Como towns. It gives an at-a-glance view in 60 seconds, with deep dives only when the visitor asks for them. This file collects the text and structure of the six prototype screens as they stand, so it can be read, shared or reused without the prototype.

**Status.** Prototype with demo content. Texts are paraphrased from public tourism sources and still need verification on site. Photos are placeholders (marked `[PHOTO …]`), pending copyright-safe images. Items in square brackets are open: prices, distances, timetables.

**Language.** The app copy below is quoted as written (English). Notes are in Italian where they describe design choices.

---

## 1. Idea in breve

- **Problema.** Il turista straniero non distingue ciò che è locale da ciò che è adattato per lui; i piatti si somigliano ovunque e i luoghi famosi si affollano.
- **Obiettivi.** Fare cultura (cibo, luoghi, persone), contrastare overtourism e appiattimento dei menu, raccogliere dati leggeri e consensuali utili ai comuni.
- **Quattro sezioni, nomi in assonanza:** **See** (i luoghi), **Eat** (il cibo), **Meet** (le persone), **Street** (come arrivare e muoversi).
- **Passaporto e timbri:** si guadagnano esplorando, non scrivendo.
- **Principio sui dati:** una sola email come dato personale; il resto sono risposte a un tocco, con il motivo spiegato accanto; al comune arrivano solo totali aggregati.

## 3. See — What to see in Bellano

Tabs: **Overview · Photos · Walk**

### Overview
Bellano is small and most of it is on foot. These are the places worth your time.

- **Orrido di Bellano** *(Start here)* — The Pioverna stream carved this narrow gorge over roughly 15 million years, and it sits right in Bellano — suspended walkways let you walk the ravine itself, past rock walls, plunge pools and waterfalls, in about 30 minutes. *Pin.*
  - Practical (expandable): Entry [price to confirm] · Visit length About 30 minutes · Season Year-round, limited hours in winter · Best time Late afternoon is calmer (7 in 10 visitors arrive before 11:00, demo figure) · Suitable for Families with children · From the station [distance to confirm]
  - **17th-century local colour:** "Orrore di un'orrenda orrendezza" — Sigismondo Boldoni, 17th-century physician, philosopher and poet from Bellano, on the gorge.
- **Ca' del Diavol** *(Gorge entrance)* — The tower at the gorge entrance, now a multimedia centre with VR installations on the story of the stream. The legend behind its name is in Meet › Stories.
- **The old centre** *(Old town)* — A well-preserved old town, with alleys that run down to the water and few shops.
- **The lakefront** *(Lake)* — The promenade where most of the town's bars, restaurants and cafés are. In summer there is a paid lido and a small free public beach.
- **Monumento a Tommaso Grossi** *(Monument)* — A white marble monument to the poet on the lakefront, inaugurated in 1876 and made by Antonio Tantardini. The inscription is signed by Alessandro Manzoni. *Pin.*
- **Santi Nazaro e Celso** *(Church)* — Piazza San Giorgio. A National Monument: a 14th-century Gothic church with a façade of black and white marble bands, a rose window decorated in majolica terracotta and 15th–16th-century frescoes inside. *Pin.*
- **Chiesa di Santa Marta** *(Church)* — Piazza Santa Marta. Holds the *Compianto sul Cristo morto*, eight painted wooden statues from the late 1400s, attributed to Giovanni Angelo del Maino. *Pin.*
- **Chiesa di San Rocco** *(Chapel)* — A 15th-century chapel later given 18th-century styling, turned into a memorial to the war dead in 1969. Two First World War bombs stand at the entrance.
- **Museo Giancarlo Vitali** *(Museum)* — Via Manzoni 50, on the ground floor of Palazzo Lorla. Over 100 paintings by Giancarlo Vitali, set up by the Comune together with the association ArchiViVitali. *Pin.*
- **San Nicolao Arte Contemporanea** *(Gallery)* — Via San Nicolao 9. Contemporary art exhibitions on the ground floor and, upstairs, the permanent collection of Danilo Vitali. *Pin.*

## 4. Eat — What Bellano eats

Tabs: **Dishes · Where · Photos**

### Dishes
Six local specialities. Where we have a confirmed venue, tap the pin for directions.

1. **Missoltini con polenta** *(Signature)* — Lake shad (agone), salted and sun-dried whole, then warmed and served with polenta. Salty, dense and unmistakably local. *Venue to confirm.*
2. **Pizzoccheri tirati a mano** — Hand-cut buckwheat tagliatelle with cabbage, potato and melted cheese — a Valtellina classic that La Darsena has served in Bellano since 1967. *Pin: La Darsena.*
3. **Risotto al pesce persico** — Rice cooked in butter and topped with pan-fried perch fillets — a staple of lake cuisine. *Pin: Trattoria del Ponte.*
4. **Trippa della Pesa Vegia** *(Jan 5 festival)* — Tripe in broth, tied to Pesa Vegia, Bellano's winter festival held every January 5 — time your visit around it to try it. *Venue to confirm.*
5. **Miascia** — A very moist bread-and-cornmeal cake: stale bread soaked in milk, with apples, pears, raisins and pine nuts — a classic dolce povero. *Venue to confirm.*
6. **Olio DOP di Biosio** — Produced in Biosio, the hillside hamlet above town, and described as Europe's northernmost olive oil (DOP) — worth asking for by name. *Pin: Biosio hamlet.*

### Where
- **La Darsena** — In a former filanda on the lakefront · Valtellina cuisine, hand-made pizzoccheri, open kitchen
- **Crotto di Biosio** — Traditional crotto · Lombard cuisine
- **Trattoria del Ponte** — Historic family-run trattoria · perch risotto
- **Taverna Malanotte** — Italian and European cuisine
- **Pasticceria Lorla** *(Don't skip)* — One of Bellano's best-known pastry shops, noted for its panettone. Worth the trip on its own.

## 5. Meet — Who made Bellano

Tabs: **People · Stories · Trail**

### People
A town of about 3,000 people, often called the town of artists.

**Then**
- **Tommaso Grossi** (1790–1853) — Poet and novelist born in Bellano, part of Alessandro Manzoni's circle in Milan.
- **Sigismondo Boldoni** (17th century) — Physician, philosopher and poet. His line about the Orrido is still the best-known description of it.
- **Also from Bellano** — The writers Antonio Balbiani and Luciano Lombardi.

**Now**
- **Andrea Vitali** *(Start here)* — Novelist who sets his books in Bellano, with its people and traditions. His voice narrates the town's visitor app.
- **Giancarlo Vitali** (1929–2018) — Painter born in Bellano. Over 100 of his paintings, made across about seventy years, hang in the town's museum.
- **Velasco Vitali** — Painter, and Giancarlo's son.
- **Danilo Vitali** (born 1935) — Sculptor born in Bellano. His copper fish hang from the ceiling at San Nicolao Arte Contemporanea.
- **Carlo Borlenghi** — Photographer whose images illustrate the town's visitor app.

### Stories
- **Pesa Vegia** *(Every Jan 5)* — A re-enactment of a dispute over weights and measures under Spanish rule: a delegation from Bellano went to Como to ask that the old standards be kept, and won. Today it brings a procession of the Magi, a race of the "pese" through the streets, the governor arriving by boat, the edict read from the Town Hall balcony and a bonfire on the pier. It has been held for more than four centuries.
- **The Devil's House** — The Ca' del Diavol is a three-storey pentagonal tower at the gorge entrance, known since the early 1600s. Its origins are uncertain. The name comes from frescoes of mythological figures and a satyr, which local lore tied to the devil.
- **A treasure in the gorge** — A local tale says a Taino warrior lies buried with an enormous treasure deep in the Orrido.
- **A gorge that worked** — From the 16th century the stream's force drove forges that worked iron from the Valsassina mines. Later it powered silk mills and a cotton mill.
- **Writers on the Orrido** — Stendhal mentions it in his *Viaggio in Italia*. Johann Jakob Wetzel and Boldoni also wrote about it.

### Trail
Places tied to the people in this section. Tap a pin for directions.

- **Tommaso Grossi** → **Monumento a Tommaso Grossi** — On the lakefront. White marble, inaugurated in 1876, made by Antonio Tantardini. The inscription is signed by Alessandro Manzoni.
- **Sigismondo Boldoni and other writers** → **Orrido di Bellano** — Boldoni, Stendhal and Wetzel all wrote about the gorge.
- **Giancarlo Vitali** → **Museo Giancarlo Vitali** — Via Manzoni 50, in Palazzo Lorla. Over 100 of his paintings.
- **Danilo Vitali** → **San Nicolao Arte Contemporanea** — Via San Nicolao 9. His copper fish hang from the ceiling.

## 6. Street — Getting here, getting around

Tabs: **Arrive · Around · Plan**

### Arrive
Three ways in.

- **By train** *(Slow traveller)* — Regional trains run direct from Milano Centrale to Bellano-Tartavalle Terme on the Lecco–Tirano line, in about 1 h 10. Check the current timetable before you go.
  - Pins (Google Maps directions, earn "Follow the pin"): Bellano station
- **By boat** *(Slow traveller)* — Navigazione Laghi runs seasonal lake services that include Bellano, with excursions linking Como, Bellano and Bellagio. Dates and times change by season: [timetable to confirm].
  - Pins (Google Maps directions, earn "Follow the pin"): Bellano pier
- **By car** — Bellano is on the SS36 Lecco–Colico road. Parking near the station is limited, so arrive early or leave the car elsewhere. [parking details to confirm]
  - Pins (Google Maps directions, earn "Follow the pin"): Station car park

**One tap — earns a stamp**

- Why we ask: pooled with other visitors, it helps the Comune plan transport.

### Around
The town is small enough to see on foot. The lake and the hills are the only reason to go further.

- **On foot in town** — The BAC route links the gorge, the museums and the churches. See › Walk for the five stops with directions.
  - Pins (Google Maps directions, earn "Follow the pin"): Orrido
- **Sentiero del Viandante** *(Hike)* — The stage from Dervio through Bellano (past the Orrido) to Varenna is about 12 km, roughly 5 hours without stops, with about 620 m of climbing. The path is part of the Cammini d'Italia network and marked with orange plates.
  - Pins (Google Maps directions, earn "Follow the pin"): Dervio station, Varenna
- **To Varenna by train** — Trains from Bellano reach Varenna in 4–6 minutes: an easy way to shorten or end the hike.
  - Pins (Google Maps directions, earn "Follow the pin"): Varenna station

### Plan
A simple way to put it together.

- **A half day, with Varenna** — Spend the morning in Bellano (gorge, old town, lunch), then take the short train to Varenna for Villa Monastero or the Castello di Vezio.
  - Pins (Google Maps directions, earn "Follow the pin"): Orrido, Villa Monastero, Castello di Vezio
- **Check the calendar** *(Before you go)* — Pesa Vegia is on January 5. The Orrido has seasonal opening and limited hours in winter, so check before you travel.
  - Pins (Google Maps directions, earn "Follow the pin"): Orrido
- **Leave the car** — Parking near the station is limited. The train and the boat both arrive within walking distance of the old town. [to confirm on site]
  - Pins (Google Maps directions, earn "Follow the pin"): Station car park

Earned stamps are drawn as a double-ring seal; locked ones are dashed, with the way to earn them written underneath.

### Your Bellano day card
- Locked until **4 stamps** are earned: "{n} to go. It builds itself from your stamps — nothing to fill in."
- Unlocked: **Your day in Bellano** — "Built from what you opened and tapped. A shareable version is the next step."

### How this works
Stamps come from what you open and tap. The few questions we ask take one tap each, and every one says why. Answers are pooled with other visitors to help the Comune plan transport and flows — never shown on their own.

A "Reset demo stamps" button exists in the prototype only.
